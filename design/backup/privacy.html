Privacy policy

Effective 5 November 2026

Purpose and operator

Recipe DB Backup is operated by the owner of this Recipe DB installation for personal backup and recovery. It is not offered as a service for other Google account holders. The operator controls the backup account, deployment, and recovery copies.

Data processed

Backups contain authoritative recipe records and associated stored information, including ingredients, preparation instructions, tags, ratings and comments, inspiration links, and current recipe images and thumbnails. Records can include identifiers linking contributions to application users. Backup manifests contain release information, timestamps, file inventories, sizes, and integrity hashes.

Google authorization supplies OAuth credentials for the operator's Drive connection. These are stored privately and separately from backup archives. Application secrets, kubeconfigs, and deployment credentials are also excluded from the archives.

Google Drive permissions and use

The integration requests https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file. It uses this permission to create backup folders and files, read them back to verify integrity, download recovery copies, and delete eligible old backup files. It does not request full access to the operator's Drive.

Google user data is used only for backup, integrity verification, retention, and recovery. It is not sold, used for advertising, supplied to AI training, or shared with unrelated third parties. Google processes files stored in Drive under its own privacy policy.

Storage, security, and retention

Backup folders remain private in the operator's Google Drive. Temporary capture and packaging files and operational status are stored privately on the backup host; recovery downloads may also be held on the operator's workstation. Backups have no additional client-side encryption, so access to the Drive account or downloaded archives can expose their contents.

The configured retention policy keeps the newest 30 verified backup points. Unchanged data may skip a new upload, so this is not a promise of deletion after 30 days. Pruning follows a verified upload; failures can leave older points retained. Local recovery and diagnostic copies must be removed separately by the operator.

Revoking access and deleting data

The operator can revoke the backup client's access in Google Account connections. Revocation prevents further authorized access but does not itself delete existing backups. To remove those, stop the backup schedule, delete the backup files in Drive and empty its trash, then remove local capture, recovery, and credential copies as appropriate. Deleting a recipe in the application does not immediately remove it from existing backups.

These informational pages

These static pages add no analytics, tracking scripts, or cookies and do not read backup data or initiate Google authorization. The hosting infrastructure may record ordinary HTTP access information, such as IP addresses and requested paths, in operational logs.

Questions and changes

For technical questions, contact the maintainer through the Recipe DB repository. Public issues must not contain personal data, backup contents, or credentials. The support email shown on the Google consent screen is the private contact channel for this OAuth integration.

Changes to this policy will be published here with an updated effective date.
